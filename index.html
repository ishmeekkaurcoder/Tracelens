TraceLens

Draw anything by tracing it in AR.

Your phone's camera shows the paper, and your picture floats on top of it, see-through. Turn any photo into an outline, line it up on the page and trace it with a real pencil.

1Place your phone 25–30 cm above the paper
2Pick a picture and switch on Outline
3Move, resize, lock, and trace
